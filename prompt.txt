\subsubsection{Northern Vietnam — Nghia Do, Hanoi}

The Nghia Do AERONET station (21.048°N, 105.800°E) recorded 13{,}172 quality-assured
observations (Feb 2022--Apr 2026) with a mean AOD$_{550}$ of 0.699. Among Level-2
products, VIIRS NOAA-20 achieved the strongest agreement ($R = 0.915$,
$R^2 = 0.837$, RMSE $= 0.271$, Bias $= +0.022$, \%EE $= 65.0$\%, $N = 103$),
using the nearest-pixel extraction (distance $\leq 25$~km).
MODIS MCD19A2 yielded $R = 0.856$ ($R^2 = 0.733$) with a negative bias
of $-0.146$ (RMSE~$= 0.295$; \%EE $= 56.9$\%; $N = 239$). Himawari-8 Level-2
contributed the largest sample ($N = 9{,}756$) but the weakest instantaneous
correlation ($R = 0.701$, RMSE $= 0.481$, \%EE $= 34.5$\%).
For the aggregated product, Himawari-8 Level-3 achieved $R = 0.869$
($R^2 = 0.756$, $N = 182$), though with the largest negative bias ($-0.316$)
and a low \%EE of 24.7\%, suggesting systematic underestimation of daily mean AOD.
All polar-orbit sensors exhibit negative biases consistent with
cloud-screening-induced removal of high-AOD retrievals during the summer monsoon
(Table~\ref{tab:hanoi_validation}).

\subsubsection{Central Vietnam}

Central Vietnam lacks a permanent AERONET station. Inter-sensor agreement between
MODIS and Himawari-8 provides a consistency proxy: $R^2 = 0.474$ with a positive
bias of $+0.118$ ($N = 480$), substantially weaker than the Northern result
($R^2 = 0.621$, Bias $= -0.038$, $N = 1{,}712$). This degradation reflects complex
orographic terrain, heterogeneous aerosol compositions (maritime, biomass burning,
and urban), and Himawari-8's sensitivity to coastal surface reflectance artefacts.
Establishing a permanent AERONET station near Đà Nẵng or Huế is strongly
recommended to constrain these uncertainties.

\subsubsection{Southern Vietnam — Bac Lieu}

The Bac Lieu AERONET station (9.30°N, 105.70°E) recorded 15{,}690 measurements
(Apr 2022--Aug 2025) with a mean AOD$_{550}$ of 0.212. Himawari-8 Level-2 achieved
$R = 0.733$ (RMSE $= 0.150$, Bias $= -0.021$, \%EE $= 53.6$\%) over 14{,}092
co-located observations---a marked improvement over the previous subset
($N = 4{,}142$), confirming robust performance of the geostationary sensor in
low-AOD maritime environments. MODIS MCD19A2 showed weaker performance
($R = 0.411$, $N = 413$), likely due to challenges over highly reflective
agricultural surfaces. VIIRS NOAA-20 achieved
$R = 0.845$ ($R^2 = 0.714$, RMSE $= 0.143$, Bias $= +0.085$, \%EE $= 45.4$\%,
$N = 207$).
Himawari-8 Level-3 achieved the highest correlation at this site
($R = 0.824$, RMSE $= 0.120$, \%EE $= 67.8$\%, $N = 295$),
demonstrating that temporal aggregation is particularly beneficial in low-AOD
environments where scan-to-scan noise dominates (Table~\ref{tab:validation_metrics}).
